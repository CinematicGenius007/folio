# Getting started

Folio is a free, open-source browser extension that turns the bookmarks your browser already keeps into a fast, keyboard-first library. It does not keep its own copy: it reads and writes your browser's **native bookmarks**, so everything you save also appears in the bookmark bar and syncs the way it always has.

## What you get

- **Instant search** over titles, links, tags, notes and folder paths, with fuzzy matching.
- **Nested folders** you can walk with the keyboard, with a breadcrumb and a folder switcher.
- **Tags and notes** on any bookmark, stored on your device.
- **Undo for deletes.** Deleted bookmarks are kept for 30 days.
- **No account, no server, no telemetry.**

It works in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc, Dia and other Chromium browsers, and in Firefox and Zen.

## Three ways in

| Where      | How to open it           | Good for                                 |
| ---------- | ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- |
| Popup      | Toolbar icon, or `Alt+S` | Saving the page you are on into a folder |
| Full page  | `Alt+Shift+F`            | Searching, organising, tidying up        |
| Side panel | `Alt+Shift+B`            | Keeping your library beside your tabs    |

## Your first five minutes

1. [Install Folio](/docs/install) and pin it to the toolbar.
2. Open any page and press `Alt+S`. Pick a folder with a number key, or press `/` to search every folder. Press `Enter` to save.
3. Press `Alt+Shift+F` to open the full page. Type to search, use `J` and `K` to move, `Enter` to open.
4. Press `C` to jump to any folder by typing part of its name, and `H` to go back up.
5. Press `?` to see every shortcut.

Folio creates one ordinary folder named **Inbox** under "Other bookmarks" for quick saves. Rename or delete it any time.
